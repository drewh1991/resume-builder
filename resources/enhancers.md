# Enhancers

Enhancers are additional skills and qualifications that complement the key achievements
and experience sections of a resume. They highlight specific strengths and capabilities
that can enhance a candidate's profile.

Use these enhancers to make the resume template more compelling and to provide a more
comprehensive view of the candidate's qualifications. You can use them in
key_achievements or experience and enhance your understanding of the experience that
should apply to each role.

- Technical leadership through coding with experience mentoring engineers via pair programming, code reviews, and collaborative problem solving.
- Strong technical communication skills for explaining complex implementations and aligning stakeholders.
- Collaborative mindset with intellectual curiosity and commitment to technical excellence through hands-on delivery.
- Expert-level SQL, Python, and modern data engineering framework experience with a focus on maintainable, scalable systems.
